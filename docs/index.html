The arrangement

We don't charge anything.

You get your home lived in and your animals looked after. We get a place to stay while we're in the country. That's the whole arrangement — no fee, no daily rate, nothing changing hands.

We only take sits of a month or longer. Shorter stays don't really work for either side. It takes most animals a week just to decide we're alright, and we'd rather be somewhere long enough to be genuinely useful.

What to expect

We're home most of the day.
We're both software engineers and we work remotely, so your animals are rarely on their own. Most of our downtime is spent quietly at our laptops, usually with something asleep nearby.
As many updates as you want.
Some people want a photo every day. Some would rather hear nothing unless something's wrong. Both are completely fine — just tell us which.
The house the way you left it.
Clean, tidy, plants watered, garden looked after. We treat other people's homes the way we'd treat our own, and we sort out small problems ourselves rather than leaving them for you to come home to.
Your car, if you're happy for us to use it.
We're both licensed drivers and we don't own a vehicle. We'd genuinely prefer to use yours if you're willing — for the grocery run, for a vet trip if one comes up, and for taking the dog somewhere more interesting than the same block every day. We've done this before with no issues. If you'd rather we didn't, we'll manage on foot, on public transport, and by rideshare.
Non-smokers, and quiet.
Neither of us smokes. We're not going to have people over or throw anything in your living room.

Who we are

We've been nomadic since 2016. We're both software engineers, and we generally settle in one area for three to six months before moving somewhere new. Most recently we spent two years living and travelling around Asia.

Dan grew up with animals in the house — a German Shepherd called London who went everywhere with him, then two cats through his twenties. Danica looked after turtles, hamsters, mice and birds.

The catch with living the way we do is that you can't really keep a pet. That's the honest reason we started house sitting: we wanted animals around again.

Long before any of this — 2013 and 2014.

We've spent the last couple of years chasing ski seasons and hiking trails — wonderful, but not very compatible with having animals around.

We miss it. So we're heading back to New Zealand and Australia, and looking for our next long sit.

Back, because we've done four sits between the two countries already — Nico in Taupō, Frankie in Auckland, Millie in Adelaide and Rusty in Brisbane. We know the flights, the seasons, and how far it is to the shops.
